Data Engineer Takeaway for Triangle Pipelines:
  • Dimension Keys: Loss triangles are aggregated along (Underwriting_Year or Accident_Year) on the vertical axis and Development_Lag_Months = DATEDIFF('month', Loss_Date, Evaluation_Date) on the horizontal.
  • Triangle Grain: Always partition by Line_of_Business (LOB), Reinsurance_Treaty_Type, and Gross_vs_Ceded_vs_Net. Mixing long-tail casualty with property cat will break the actuarial link ratio algorithms.
  • Restatement & Snapshots: Actuaries require frozen historical triangle snapshots (month-end / quarter-end valuation dates) to satisfy SOX and external audit requirements.

Arch Re Data Engineering Architecture & Core Pipeline Blueprint

An overview of the end-to-end data pipelines, ingestion sources, core schemas, and reconciliations you will encounter as a Senior Data Engineer at Arch Re.

1. Source Ingestion Layer (Bronze)

Messy, disparate external & internal feeds:

  • Broker Bordereaux: Excel/CSV/PDF files sent by Aon, Guy Carpenter, Gallagher Re for monthly/quarterly premium & claims.
  • ACORD XML / e-Messaging: Standardized XML/JSON messages (B2B Ruschlikon standards).
  • Underwriting & Pricing Systems: Internal pricing models (Python/R, actuarial workbooks, RMS/AIR catastrophe event loss tables).
  • Policy Administration Systems (PAS): Treaty slips, endorsements, contract inception/expiration dates.

2. Normalized Enterprise Model (Silver)

Cleansed, validated, and reconciled data lakehouse tables:

  • dim_treaty_contract: SCD Type 2 handling endorsements and mid-term limit changes.
  • dim_contract_section_layer: Attachment points, limits, reinstated terms, ceding commissions.
  • fact_premium_movement: Gross Written, Unearned, Ceded, Ceding Commission, and Pipeline estimates.
  • fact_claim_movement: Paid claims, case reserves, ALAE/ULAE expense movements, salvage/subrogation.
  • dim_cat_event: Peril, geographic footprint, industry loss identifier (PCS / PERILS AG).

3. Analytical & Regulatory Marts (Gold)

High-performance columnar cubes & actuarial data stores:

  • Actuarial Reserving Mart: Aggregated cumulative loss triangles by AY, LOB, Treaty for IBNR estimation.
  • Underwriting Performance: Combined ratios, burning costs, loss ratios by broker, cedant, and class of business.
  • Bermuda BMA (BSCR) & Solvency II Mart: Capital adequacy calculations, technical reserves, counterparty credit risk.
  • US GAAP & IFRS 17 Engine: Contractual Service Margin (CSM), discount rates, and risk adjustment calculations.
Senior Data Engineer Mission-Critical Rules at Arch Re:
  1. Tri-Currency Accounting: Never aggregate monetary values without normalizing currency! Every record has: Original_Currency (e.g. EUR, JPY), Settlement_Currency (GBP), and Base_Ledger_Currency (USD) with conversion date rates.
  2. Late Arriving Claims & Restatements: A claim occurring in 2021 can be reported in 2024. Your pipelines must distinguish between Accident_Date, Reported_Date, and Accounting_Entry_Date.
  3. Contractual Cascades: A catastrophe claim on a cedant's policy passes through a Quota Share treaty, then an Excess of Loss tower, and finally gets ceded out to Arch Re's Retrocessionaires. Lineage tracking is vital.
